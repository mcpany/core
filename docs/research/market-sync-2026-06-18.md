# Market Sync: 2026-06-18
## Ecosystem Shifts
- **OpenClaw (v3.2):** Introduced Intent-Boundary Telemetry (IBT) for subagent lifecycle monitoring.
- **Claude Code:** Cognitive Proof-of-Work (CPoW) now standard for high-privileged local operations.
- **Gemini CLI:** Shift towards "Active Tool Presence" requiring persistent mTLS handshakes.

## Pain Points & Vulnerabilities
- **Fragment-Ghosting:** Abandoned subagent tool-locks causing host resource exhaustion.
- **Boundary Smearing:** Standard context inheritance leading to accidental PII leakage in multi-agent swarms.
