- **Finding**: Gemini CLI v0.59.0 now embeds non-repudiable watermarks into reasoning fragments to support PPRP.
- **Context**: These watermarks allow for hardware-attested proof that a specific reasoning step was generated by a verified model instance, preventing "Chain-of-Thought Spoofing."
- **Significance**: Directly aligns with the **Reasoning-Path Watermark (RPW) Validator** strategic priority.
