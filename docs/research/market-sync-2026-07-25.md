### Menlo Security 2026 Predictions
- **AI Agents as Insider Threats**: Autonomous agents executing tasks on behalf of users are identified as a major new "Insider Threat" vector.
- **The "Audit Log Tax"**: SaaS vendors are increasingly charging premiums for high-quality, high-fidelity audit logs, hindering security teams' ability to investigate breaches effectively.
- **NHI Lifecycle Risks**: Orphaned and overprivileged Non-Human Identity (NHI) accounts (e.g., abandoned SaaS-to-SaaS connections) are proliferating, leaving valid tokens unmonitored.

## Critical Pain Points
- **Accountability Gap**: The cost of audit logs creates a "tax" on visibility, leaving many organizations guessing during breach investigations.
- **NHI Proliferation**: The explosion of task-specific agents is outpacing current identity management and revocation tools.
- **Autonomy Risks**: Gartner projects 40% of enterprise apps will embed agents by 2026, scaling the attack surface for prompt injection and data exfiltration.
