- **Significance**: Confirms the urgent need for **Monotonic Coordination Anchoring** and **Echo-Immune Fragments** in the Universal Agent Bus.

### 3. Gemini CLI: Epistemic Watermarking

- **Significance**: Validates the **Reasoning Confidence Scoring (RCS) Gateway** and **Epistemic Attestation** features.
