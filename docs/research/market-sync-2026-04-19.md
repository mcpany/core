*   **Finding:** Point-in-time boot checks are being phased out by enterprise security teams in favor of "Continuous Attestation." This shift addresses vulnerabilities where an environment is compromised mid-session.
*   **Implication:** Our "Resident Integrity Monitor (RIM)" and the proposed "Continuous Sandbox Policy Verifier" are now the primary competitive differentiators for enterprise deployments.

*   **Implication:** There is a critical market need for a "Cross-Framework Attestation Bridge" that can provide shared, low-latency trust leases across multiple subagents.
