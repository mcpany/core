**Impact:** Solves the "Cognitive Stall" problem in deep speculative swarms by preventing the attention window from being flooded with redundant reasoning paths.

## Autonomous Agent Pain Points
- **Intent Drift:** The cumulative divergence of specialized subagents from the parent's core mission during long reasoning loops.
- **Stylometric Collision:** The risk of identity confusion in horizontal meshes when multiple agents use similar reasoning frameworks.
- **Speculative Overload:** The performance tax of maintaining large numbers of hardware-attested speculative branches.
