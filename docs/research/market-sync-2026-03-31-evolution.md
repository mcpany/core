# Market Sync: 2026-03-31 (Strategic Evolution)

## Ecosystem Updates

### ClawHub Marketplace: Supply Chain Infection
A comprehensive audit of ClawHub has revealed a massive supply chain infection, with researchers identifying 341 malicious skills. This represents an infection rate of approximately 12% across the marketplace, including skills that initially passed "Burn-In" tests by mimicking legitimate behavior. This discovery underscores the critical need for continuous behavioral attestation rather than one-time verification.

## Strategic Significance
This finding confirms that the current "Gatekeeper" model for tool discovery is insufficient. Security must move from point-of-installation verification to continuous, mesh-wide behavioral reputation tracking.
